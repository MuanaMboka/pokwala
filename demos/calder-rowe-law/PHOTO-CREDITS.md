All photography in `assets/photos/` is from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial and non-commercial use,
no permission or attribution required. Attribution is recorded here as good practice.

The licence does not allow selling unmodified copies, building a competing stock service, or
implying that an identifiable person endorses a business.

> **Before a client site goes live:** replace these with the firm's own office, team and
> building photography. Stock imagery is scaffolding.

> **No lawyer portraits on purpose.** The "lawyers" on this demo are fictional, so they get
> monogram badges instead of stock faces. A real face next to an invented name and "LSO
> licence" would read as a fabricated professional. The two stock photos that show people
> (`couple-signing`, `handshake-office`) are used only as illustrations of a practice area,
> never beside a name or a review.

## Why these

The set sits in warm wood, paper and muted blue-grey mid-tones, so `--navy`, `--sand` and
`--gold` sit on top without a colour fight. Rejected so the next vertical doesn't re-litigate it:

- Lady Justice statues and gavels: the most-used legal stock images, and several come from
  real law firms' own Unsplash accounts.
- Parliament Hill: recognisable, but it says "government", not "your lawyer", and doesn't
  belong on suburban area pages.
- Any portrait of a single person: see the note above about fictional lawyers.
