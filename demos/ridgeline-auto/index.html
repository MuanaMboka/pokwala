Services

What we do, and what we’ll tell you about it.

Four things make up most of what comes through the bay. All of them start the same way: we look, we quote, you decide.

Diagnostics

Dealer-grade scan tools, and a technician who explains the fault code in plain language instead of handing you a printout.

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, rotors, struts and alignment. Most jobs booked in the morning are finished the same afternoon.

Scheduled Maintenance

Oil, filters, fluids and inspections, timed to your vehicle’s actual service interval rather than a sticker on the windscreen.

Warranty Work

Every repair we carry out is covered for twelve months on parts and labour. If it comes back, it comes back to us.

Our promise

Nothing gets touched until you’ve seen the price in writing.
