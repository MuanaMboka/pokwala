# Photo credits and licence notes

All photography in `assets/photos/` is from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license).

**What the licence allows:** free commercial and non-commercial use, no permission needed,
no attribution required. Attribution is appreciated and is recorded here as good practice.

**What it does not allow:** selling unmodified copies of the photos, or compiling them to
build a competing stock service. Using them inside a website template that is sold as a
template is fine, because the value sold is the template, not the photograph.

> **Before a client site goes live:** replace these with real photos of that client's own
> shop, bay and technicians. Stock imagery is scaffolding. The whole trust argument of an
> auto-repair site rests on the photos being the actual facility — see `PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.md`.

## Why these five

They were picked as a set, not individually. All five are tungsten-lit interiors with deep
shadows and warm mid-tones, so they sit together on one page without looking like a stock
grab-bag. The dark bias is deliberate: it gives the near-black palette something to hold on
to, and leaves unlit areas in frame where headline type can sit directly on the photo
without a scrim fighting it.

Rejected candidates and why, so the next vertical does not re-litigate this:

- Bright multi-bay dealership shop — cluttered, and carried signage in another language.
- Daylight fuse-box repair — cool blue daylight, clashed with the rest of the set.
- Audi R8 engine bay — a supercar reads as a specialist tuner, not a neighbourhood shop.
