Questions

Trades website FAQ

Do I need a page for each city I serve?

For the main areas you want work from, yes. Each area page should say something specific about the work you do there, not just swap the city name.

Can customers book online?

Yes. Trades Pro includes a quote or booking form. If you already use a booking tool, I can link to it or embed it where that tool allows. On Wix, built-in online booking needs Wix's Core plan ($29/month, billed by Wix).

Will it work with my Google Business Profile?

Yes. I set up or clean up your profile and connect it to your website, since both help you show up on Google Maps.

I'm a one-person business. Is this overkill?
