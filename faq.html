SEO

Yes, more than ever. Every day, people search Google for a plumber, a roofer, or a dentist near them. What has changed is that AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews now also recommend businesses, and they tend to recommend the ones with strong SEO.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) gets your website ranking on Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your business mentioned and recommended by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Both are built on the same foundations: good content, clear information, and a trustworthy website.

Most businesses see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, with early wins sometimes in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive keywords can take 6 to 12 months of consistent work. SEO is a foundation you build, not a switch you flip.

Every site I build already includes the SEO basics, at no extra cost. For more, a one-time SEO audit with fixes is $299, and ongoing monthly SEO starts at $249 for an existing site, or $299 as part of a site care plan for a site I built.
