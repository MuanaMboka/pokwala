SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the practice of getting your website to rank on Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the newer practice of getting your business mentioned and recommended by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. When someone asks ChatGPT "who is the best contractor in Ottawa?", GEO is what determines whether your business gets named. The good news: strong SEO and strong GEO are built on the same foundations: good content, clear information, and a trustworthy website.

Local SEO is a set of practices that help your business show up when people nearby search for what you offer. It includes: optimising your Google Business Profile so you appear on the map, making sure your website mentions the right locations and services, building citations (listings on directories like Yelp, Yellow Pages, and industry sites), and earning reviews that signal trust to Google. For an Ottawa tradesperson, local SEO is often more valuable than broad SEO because your customers are always nearby; they are not searching from Tokyo.

A professional SEO service does not make guarantees it cannot keep. Google's algorithm is not something any agency controls; no one can guarantee a specific ranking. What a trustworthy agency can guarantee is the quality of the work: proper technical setup, well-researched content, clean backlink practices, and honest monthly reporting. Red flags to watch for: agencies that will not explain what they do, lock you into long contracts without results, or focus on metrics that do not translate to actual business (like raw traffic numbers with no leads).

Google reads your website the same way a person would, looking for clear, useful information.
