What I believe

How I think about the work.

01

Honesty over hype.

SEO takes time. I will never promise page one rankings in two weeks. What I will promise is transparent work, honest timelines, and a clear explanation of everything I do.

02

Results you can understand.

Monthly reports in plain language, not a wall of numbers that requires a data scientist to interpret. If your rankings improved you will see it. If something is not working I will tell you why.
