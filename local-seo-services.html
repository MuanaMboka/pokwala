What local SEO actually does for you

Most small businesses don't need to rank across the country. They need to show up when someone within driving distance is ready to call. That happens in two places:

  • The map results (Google's local pack). The three businesses shown with the map. This is driven mostly by your Google Business Profile, your reviews and how consistently your business details appear across the web.
  • The regular results underneath. Driven by your website: whether it has a clear page for each service you offer and the areas you serve, and whether Google can read it properly.

Local SEO services work on both, because a strong profile pointing to a weak website (or the reverse) leaves calls on the table.

Local SEO FAQ

How much do local SEO services cost?

Pokwala's Local SEO plan is $599 a month in USD, with no setup fee. It covers Google Business Profile management, on-page SEO, citations, review monitoring, one article a month and a monthly report.

What's the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular SEO aims to rank a site for searches anywhere. Local SEO targets people searching near you, which means the map results, your Google Business Profile and pages built around the areas you serve all matter as much as the website itself.

Do I need a physical address to do local SEO?

No. Service-area businesses like plumbers, cleaners and contractors can hide their address on Google and list the areas they serve instead. The approach is a bit different, and I'll set it up correctly either way.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody honestly can. Google decides rankings. What I guarantee is the work: done properly, every month, with clear reporting on what moved.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. There's a 3-month minimum, because the first month is setup and local SEO needs a few months to show movement. After that it's month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. Most agencies ask for 6 to 12 months; I'd rather keep you because the work is paying off.

What happens to my rankings if I stop?

You keep everything I built: pages, listings and your profile all stay in your name. Rankings can drift over time without ongoing work, but you won't lose the foundations.
