Questions

Small business web design FAQ

How long does a website take?

Most Starter sites launch in 2 to 3 weeks once I have your content. Larger packages take 3 to 4 weeks.

Do you write the content?

I write the page text with you, based on a short call about your business, and you approve every page before launch.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes, on Wix. Custom-built sites are edited by me through a care plan or on request.

What if I need more pages later?

Extra pages are $150 each, with the same SEO setup as the rest of your site.

Do you build online stores?

No. Pokwala builds websites for service businesses, not e-commerce or Shopify stores. If you already run an online store, I can help with its SEO.
