FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Ask during your free 15-minute discovery call.

Do I need to be an elite athlete to work with you?

Not at all. I work with Olympians and first-time marathoners alike. If you train with a goal in mind, performance nutrition will help you reach it.

Is coaching available online?

Yes. Most clients are coached remotely via video calls and the coaching app. In-person assessments and body composition testing are available on request.

Will I have to cut out foods I love?

No. Plans are built around foods you already enjoy. The focus is on timing, quantity and quality — not restriction.

Do you work with teams and clubs?

Yes — from academy squads to professional teams. Options include workshops, canteen audits, and ongoing embedded nutrition support.

Are supplements included?

Supplement recommendations are included in every package, and only third-party tested, sport-safe products are ever suggested.
