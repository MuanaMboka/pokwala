Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first 30 minutes are free, by phone, video or in person. You get an honest view of your options and what the next step would cost, with no obligation.

Do you offer services in French?

Yes. Elena Calder practises in English and French, and we can prepare documents in either language.

Where is your office?

Our office is downtown at 100 Example Street, Suite 410, Ottawa. It is wheelchair accessible, and paid parking is available nearby. Many clients never need to visit: most meetings and signings can be done by video.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Usually within three business days, and sooner for urgent matters like a court date, a closing, or a severance deadline. Thursday evening appointments run until 7:30 pm.
