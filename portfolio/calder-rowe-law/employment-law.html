Minimum vs. reasonable notice. Ontario’s Employment Standards Act sets a floor. Unless your contract has a valid termination clause, you are usually entitled to much more under the common law, which looks at your age, role, years of service and how hard it will be to find similar work.

Employment Law: common questions

How long do I have to sign a severance package?

Whatever deadline your employer sets is usually negotiable. Do not sign a release until you have had advice. The general limitation period to sue for wrongful dismissal in Ontario is two years, but some steps are time-sensitive, so get advice early.

Is my non-compete clause enforceable?

Ontario banned most non-compete clauses in employment agreements entered into on or after October 25, 2021, with exceptions for certain executives and the sale of a business. Non-solicit clauses are treated differently, and we can tell you whether yours is likely to hold.

Do I get severance pay or just notice?

They are different things. Under the Employment Standards Act, severance pay applies when you have five or more years of service and your employer has a payroll of at least $2.5 million. Notice of termination applies more broadly. On top of both, the common law may give you more.

Do you act for employers?

Yes, for small and mid-sized Ottawa employers, mainly on contracts, policies and terminations. We always run a conflict check first.
