Real Estate Law: common questions

Does Ottawa charge a municipal land transfer tax?

No. Only Toronto has a municipal land transfer tax. Ottawa buyers pay the Ontario land transfer tax, and eligible first-time buyers can get a refund of up to $4,000 of it, which we claim for you on closing.

When should I hire a real estate lawyer?

Ideally before you sign an offer, and at the latest as soon as the deal is firm. Earlier is better for new builds and condos, where the documents are long and the deadlines are short.

Can I cancel a pre-construction condo purchase?

In Ontario you have a 10-day cooling-off period after signing an agreement for a new condo unit, or after receiving the disclosure statement. We can review the documents inside that window.

Do I have to come to your office?

No. Most clients sign by secure video, and we can meet in person at our office if you prefer.
